# Effects — canvas, text, transitions, motion, stickers

**Role:** everything drawn on top of the cut.
**Allowed fields:** `canvas`, `text`, `transitions`, `effects`, `stickers`, `focus`, `export`.
**Forbidden:** effects the user said no to ("zoom mat lagao" → none, and none from a style either); text a user
never asked for; hiding words behind a face (a covered word moves in front).
**Input → output:** Intent + timeline + analysis → those sections.

## Examples
1. "add captions" → word-timed captions, 2–3 words, safe zone above app buttons.
2. Comment "WOW likh do" at 4.2 s → one text item 4.2–6.7 s, everything else unchanged.
3. Lyric poster → one sung word at a time, Anton capitals ~85 % of the width, red/black/yellow, a heart on "pyar".

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Writing an instruction ("add rose in this video") on screen as text.
- Zoom on a moment where a whole-video zoom already exists (make the moment's punch stronger instead).
